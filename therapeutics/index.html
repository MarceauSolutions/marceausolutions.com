How to read these. Every claim carries an evidence tag, because most confusion in this field comes from treating a marketing claim as if it were a trial result.

T1
Approved by a regulator, or Phase 3 published peer-reviewed
T2
Phase 1/2 results published peer-reviewed
T3
Preclinical, animal, or preprint
T4
Marketed or sold without controlled human efficacy data

Sourcing

Sources are peer-reviewed journals, regulatory actions, and registered trials. Vendor marketing, clinic websites, and podcast claims are excluded. Where a figure could not be verified against primary full text, the paper says so rather than presenting it as settled. Nothing is estimated, interpolated, or inferred.
