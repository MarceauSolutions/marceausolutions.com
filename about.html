An Engineer Who Builds Systems That Actually Work

I'm William Marceau, a Naples-based engineer. I build websites that book customers for Southwest Florida businesses, and I help owners figure out where AI can actually save them time.

The Backstory

As a teenager, I ran into obstacles that seemed impossible to get past. The kind that rewrite the plan for your whole life before you've had the chance to make one.

I didn't accept the plan I was handed. I decided to build a better one.

It wasn't fast and it wasn't easy. Becoming the man I am took years of doing the hard thing again and again, and learning to take any problem apart until I understood exactly how it worked. That habit carried me through a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering at FGCU, graduating with honors.

Today my day-to-day work is in industrial automation and controls: making systems do the right thing reliably, every time. I bring that same standard to every website I build. Every inquiry gets captured, nothing gets lost, and it runs without you having to think about it.

Embrace the Pain & Defy the Odds isn't a slogan to me. It's how I work.

What I Do Now

I build websites for Southwest Florida businesses that turn visitors into bookings: a fast, custom site with a booking form that sends every inquiry to the owner's phone and their own Google Sheet.

I figure out how systems work and make them better.

I also run my own operations on AI tools I've built: email, scheduling, research, follow-ups. That's why I offer business owners a free 45-minute session on where AI can save them time. What I tell you comes from using it every day, not from a sales pitch.

You work directly with me: fixed prices, a full preview before you pay the balance, and a same-day reply.
